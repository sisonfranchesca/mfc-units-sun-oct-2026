Guidelines

Everything You Need To Know!

MOVE-IN PROCESS

1. Reservation Payment:

  • To secure the unit, a reservation fee equivalent to 1 month's advance rent is required. Once you officially move in and continue with the lease, this reservation fee will automatically cover your 1st month's rent.
  • Please note: If you decide to cancel, the reservation fee is non-refundable.

2. Information Sharing & Document Preparation:

  • After you secure the reservation, the Owner will request your full personal details to draft the lease contract.
  • Simultaneously, the Owner will provide you with the necessary Move-In Documents. The unit details will already be pre-filled by the Owner, so you only need to fill out your personal information. These documents include:
    • Move-In Clearance for Tenant Form
    • Tenant Information Sheet Form
    • SRCC Gate Pass Form
    • Resident RFID Application Form (with Passport-Size Photo)
    • Notarized Contract Document (signed by the Owner/Lawyer)
  • Note: You can verify and inspect the official templates of these files by checking the Sun Residences Online Forms link.

3. Notarization:

  • Once the paperwork is filled out and signed, the documents will be processed for legal notarization.

4. Admin Submission (via Email):

  • Once notarized, the Owner will submit the documents to the building administration.
  • Processing Time: Approval takes seven (7) working days (Excluding weekends and holidays).

DAY OF MOVE-IN

1. Move-In Clearance & Gate Pass:

  • Once the admin approves the submitted documents via email, they will issue an approved, stamped Move-In Clearance. You must present this stamped copy to the building security guard on your move-in day.

2. Final Payment on-site:

  • Full payment for the 2-month security deposit and the ₱1,000 RFID fee must be settled on the day of move-in.
  • Payment Method: Bank transfer or Bank deposit.

RFID CARD

  • From the 39th floor, you need to go down to the 6th floor and to the Tower 1 (2nd floor) admin office to complete your RFID registration by bringing:
    • One (1) Passport-size photo
    • ₱150 processing fee for the RFID card
    • Valid ID for identity verification
  • While waiting for your actual RFID card to be processed and issued, the admin will provide you with a claim stub as your temporary pass to enter the premises and as official proof of payment.

FINANCIALS & UTILITY PAYMENTS

  • Included in Rent: Basic water, Wi-Fi, and condo association dues are fully covered by the landlord.
  • Electricity: Must be paid by the tenant every month on or before the 16th.
  • Split Bills: All tenant utilities (like electricity) must be split equally among all tenants, even if someone is temporarily away or on vacation.
  • Late Rent Policy:
    • Days 1–14: Grace period to pay rent.
    • After Day 14: A 10% penalty fee is applied to overdue rent.
    • Day 30: If unpaid after 30 days, automatic eviction and contract termination apply, with 100% forfeiture of the security deposit.

HOUSE RULES & SAFETY

  • Occupancy: Maximum of 4 authorized occupants only.
  • No Subleasing: You cannot sublet, transfer, or share the room/unit with unauthorized persons.
  • Strict Visitor Policy:
    • No overnight guests or sleepovers under any circumstances (causes immediate security deposit forfeiture and same-day eviction).
    • Female visitors only allowed, and only until 7:00 PM.
    • No male visitors permitted in the unit at any time.
  • Strict Prohibitions: No smoking, no pets, and no illegal acts.

UNIT CARE & MAINTENANCE

  • Wall Protection: Strictly no drilling holes, posting, taping, or sticking items on the walls that could damage paint. Tenants must pay to repair and repaint any damaged surfaces.
  • Routine Cleaning:
    • Tenants must maintain a weekly cleaning schedule. Unannounced inspections may occur; if unmaintained, a professional cleaning service will be hired at the tenants' expense.
  • Repairs Threshold:
    • Minor Repairs (< ₱3,000): Paid by the tenant.
    • Major Repairs (> ₱3,000): Handled by the landlord (unless caused by tenant negligence).
  • Mandatory Periodic Servicing (Capped at ₱1,000 max per instance):
    • Air-con cleaning: At least every 3 months.
    • Grease trap cleaning: Monthly or every 2 months.
    • Plumbing/Toilet servicing: Every 2 months.

RENEWAL & EARLY TERMINATION

  • Lease Renewal: If renewing, written notice must be sent at least 30 days before the contract ends.
  • Early Termination: If terminating early, 30 days' written notice is required, but the 2-month security deposit and RFID deposit are forfeited. The tenant must pay for the full cost of notarizing the contract as well as all associated legal paper and processing fees.

MOVE-OUT PROCESS

1. Submission of Move-Out Documents:

  • When moving out, the Owner will submit the required Move-Out Documents to the admin on your behalf:
    • SRCC Move-Out Clearance for Tenant Form
    • SRCC Gate Pass Form
  • Note: You may also review these official forms via the Sun Residences Online Forms link.

2. Surrender of RFID:

  • You are required to return your access RFID card directly to the owner or to the building admin.
  • Important: If you choose to return the RFID card to the building admin yourself, you must secure a signed proof of receipt to confirm they have officially received it.
