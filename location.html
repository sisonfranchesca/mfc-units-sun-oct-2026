Finding Your Way Around

Navigating around the area is very convenient, with plenty of public jeepneys, buses, UV Express vans, and tricycles passing through nearby streets to take you to these destinations and surrounding universities.

As routes and jeepney signboards can vary depending on where you are heading, we recommend checking your exact destination on Google Maps or asking local drivers/guards for the best direct route.
